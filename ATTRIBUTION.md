### Textures

The models were retextured and reworked for this pack, but the texture sets themselves
come from the same donor models rather than being painted from scratch. The weapon
bodies use the Kh-25ML, AS-30L and Kh-29 sets, and the two authored mounts are painted
with sets off the four aircraft above. All of those are
[KojfDiscord](https://sketchfab.com/KojfDiscord)'s work under CC BY 4.0.
