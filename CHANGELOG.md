# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - unreleased

First cut. Nothing has shipped to anyone yet.

Three weapons, with pylon and bay carriage:

- **AGM-84**, heavy optical, 20 km, locked on before release and independent afterwards.
- **AGM-57L**, laser-guided, 15 km, blast-heavy.
- **AGM-33L**, light laser-guided, 12 km, for helicopters and light attack aircraft.

Also in this version:

- Carrying any Meridian mount raises the aircraft's lased target limit to six. It is a
  floor rather than a bonus, so it does not compound with another mod that raises the
  same limit.
- The laser HUD's designation ring stops growing at 15 km, past which nothing in the pack
  can be fired. It applies to these weapons only and does not change the firing gate.
- Motor plumes and flames are drawn from the game's own effects, picked per weapon. See
  [ATTRIBUTION.md](./ATTRIBUTION.md).
